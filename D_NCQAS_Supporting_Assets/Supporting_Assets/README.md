# D-NCQAS 3.0 — optimizer diagnostics and controlled circuit search

This release completes the corrective study with learning diagnostics, tuned
generic search controls, fresh-seed inference, a molecular convergence study,
and a rewritten paper: **Optimizer Sensitivity and Candidate Selection in
Noise-Aware Quantum Circuit Search**. This is a controlled synthetic simulation
study, not a claim of hardware or state-of-the-art superiority.

## Start here

Read `QUICKSTART.md`, then `manuscript/main_paper.pdf` and
`manuscript/REVISION_RESPONSE.md`. Editable Markdown and LaTeX are included.
`docs/REVISION_PROTOCOL.md` records the prospective final analysis plan; its
SHA-256 is frozen in the final study manifest.

not vendored: initial installation needs package-index access. No GPU is needed.

## Research study

- Development: 24 optimizer runs, 48 CEM/evolution tuning runs, and 36 budget-scaling
  runs. These use seeds 100–103 and do not tune on final seeds.
- Main evaluation: seven methods, three fixed tasks, 32 new seeds = 672 runs.
  Each search method proposes 384 circuits; the analytic tree enumerates roots.
- Secondary ablations: four changes, sixteen paired seeds, five-qubit tree = 64 runs.
- Primary inference: four task-averaged paired comparisons, Holm adjusted. The
  seed is the independent unit. Planning assumptions are explicit; positive
  findings are not guaranteed or required.
- VQE: 120 fixed-ansatz fits, five matched starts, COBYLA caps 400/2000 and BFGS
  with a checked parameter-shift Jacobian. Independent stationarity and solver
  success are separate fields. All selected BFGS fits satisfy the gradient
  criterion; this does not prove global optimality.

See `outputs/analysis/tables/` for final tables and
`outputs/analysis/metadata/integrity_report.json` for verified counts. Per-job
JSONs preserve observations, parameters, configurations and diagnostics.
The main raw CSV has 220,800 observations including secondary ablations;
the VQE raw CSV has 36,120. Figures include 600-DPI PNG and vector PDF versions.
The output-only ZIP contains every phase, not just summary plots.

## Reproduction

Follow `QUICKSTART.md`. The four GHZ specs are `development_optimizer.json`,
`development_baselines.json`, `development_scaling.json` and `final_study.json`.
Phase runners skip complete identity-matched jobs and reject changed scientific
source/configurations. They do not resume an interrupted optimizer minibatch.
Use a new output root for new experiments and never run two writers on one phase.

`run_all_experiments.py` remains the small/legacy driver for the smoke notebook.
Its `publication.json` is the earlier protocol, not the revision-3 study.
The updated paper is generated by `build_revision_paper.py` from verified
revision outputs. `build_manuscript.py` is a compatibility alias for that same
verified builder, so it cannot silently restore the obsolete paper.

## Scope and attribution

GHZ graphs and noise are synthetic. Premeasurement fidelity is separate from
assignment-noisy success. There are no QPU or mitigation results. CEM and aging
evolution are independent generic algorithm adaptations, not reproductions of
specialized QAS software. VQE is fixed-ansatz validation, not learned architecture
search. Hamiltonians use compact CAS(2,2) CI encoding; LiH freezes its core and
uses active-space FCI. This differs from four-qubit Jordan–Wigner device noise.

MIT licensing and `CITATION.cff` are supplied. The paper is anonymous and venue
neutral. Authors must confirm attribution, author order, affiliations, funding,
conflicts, AI-assistance disclosure and venue rules. This is not an official
replication of the motivating coevolutionary framework.
